# Commercial Use

Ugent is free for personal, educational, research, evaluation, and
non-commercial community use.

Commercial use requires prior written permission from the Ugent Project owner.

## Uses That Need Permission

You need written permission before using Ugent for:

- paid products or services;
- SaaS, hosted, managed, or consulting services;
- production business operations;
- customer-facing workflows;
- internal workflows that support commercial activity;
- white-labeling or rebranding;
- resale or sublicensing;
- commercial products based substantially on Ugent source code.

## Requesting Permission

To request permission, contact the project owner with:

- your name or organization;
- intended use case;
- whether the use is internal, customer-facing, hosted, or redistributed;
- expected scale;
- whether you need support, custom terms, or a commercial license.

Written permission must be granted before commercial use begins.

## Separate Agreements

A separate written commercial agreement may grant rights not included in the
community license. That agreement applies only to the rights and parties stated
in it.
