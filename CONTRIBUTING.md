# Contributing to Ugent

Thanks for considering a contribution to Ugent.

## License of Contributions

By submitting a pull request, issue patch, code sample, documentation update, or
other contribution, you agree that your contribution may be used under the Ugent
Community Source License v1.0 and any future license chosen by the Ugent Project.

You confirm that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution;
- the contribution is your original work or is compatible with this project;
- the contribution does not knowingly violate third-party rights;
- you understand that commercial use of Ugent still requires written permission
  unless separately granted.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

By contributing, you certify that you have the right to submit the work under the
project's terms. If you are contributing on behalf of an employer or client, make
sure you have permission to do so.

## Project Direction

Ugent is currently maintained for personal, educational, research, evaluation,
and non-commercial community use. Commercial licensing may be handled separately.
